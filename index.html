Trail Readiness Standard

Bandaje Falls Trek: Equipment & Readiness Checklist

Navigating the 18 km rugged circuit across Shola forest tracts and steep terrain toward the 200-ft Bandaje cascade demands strict trail readiness. Use this field preparation guide to inspect your equipment before departure.

1. Footwear & Trail Traction

  • High-traction trekking shoes with deep lug patterns (wet mud and slick basalt slabs require firm grip).
  • 2 pairs of technical moisture-wicking synthetic or merino blend socks.
  • Leach repellent spray or high-concentration brine solution for dense undergrowth sections.

2. Pack & Waterproofing Essentials

  • 20L to 30L technical daypack with integrated rain cover.
  • Heavy-duty dry bags or dry-sacks for electronics and reserve clothing layers.
  • Hydration reservoir or dual reusable bottles with at least 2.5L total capacity.

3. Field Safety & Regulations

  • Hard-copy Government Photo Identification (Aadhaar or Passport) required for Forest Department entry checkpoints.
  • Dedicated headlamp (minimum 150 lumens) with spare lithium batteries.
  • Personal blister kit, ORS sachets, and compact first-aid kit.
